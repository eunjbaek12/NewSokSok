5. 앱 권한 사용 목적

  • 마이크 / 음성인식: 단어 음성 입력 기능에만 사용. 음성은 기기의 음성 인식 기능으로 글자로 바뀝니다. 앱은 음성을 저장하거나 우리 서버로 보내지 않습니다.
  • 카메라: 사진 스캔으로 단어를 추가할 때만 사용. 촬영된 이미지는 텍스트 인식 후 즉시 폐기되며 저장되지 않습니다.
  • 사진 라이브러리 (선택): 갤러리에서 단어장 사진을 불러올 때만 사용. 선택한 사진만 텍스트 인식에 활용됩니다.
  • 광고 ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Pro가 아닌 사용자에게 광고를 노출하는 데만 사용. Pro 사용자에게는 광고가 표시되지 않습니다.
  • iOS 광고 식별자(IDFA): 앱 추적 허용을 요청합니다.

5-1. Pro 구독 결제 및 환불

  • 결제 처리: Pro 구독 결제는 전적으로 앱을 설치한 스토어(Android는 Google Play, iOS는 App Store)를 통해 진행되며, 카드 정보 등 결제 수단은 각 스토어가 처리합니다.
  • 저장 항목: 본 앱은 구독 활성화에 필요한 구매 토큰(Android) 또는 거래 식별자(iOS)와 상품 ID, 만료일만 저장합니다.
  • 해지: Android — Google Play 앱 → 메뉴 → 결제 및 정기 결제 → 정기 결제. iOS — 설정 앱 → 사용자 이름 → 구독. 어느 쪽이든 언제든 해지할 수 있습니다.
  • 환불: 결제한 스토어(Google Play 또는 App Store)의 환불 정책을 따르며, 환불 요청도 해당 스토어를 통해 진행됩니다.
  • 해지 후 데이터: 구독 해지·만료 후에도 단어장·학습 데이터는 그대로 유지됩니다 (Free 한도로 자동 전환).

2. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Account identification and sign-in
  • Cloud sync of learning data across devices
  • Providing AI-based word and example generation (Google Gemini / Vertex AI)
  • Managing daily AI quotas and accruing rewarded-ad bonuses
  • Verifying Pro subscription status and payment receipts (Google Play Developer API · Apple App Store Server API)
  • Showing advertising (users without Pro, Google AdMob)
  • Receiving and answering support enquiries and reports (in-app replies)
  • Diagnosing service errors and improving quality
  • Understanding feature usage (to improve features). Feature usage counts are not used for ads or shared with third parties.

3. Retention and Deletion

  • Retention period: Until you delete your account or stop using the service.
  • Deletion method: Complete removal from the database immediately upon account deletion.
  • Legal retention: Where applicable laws require longer retention, we retain only for the period required and then delete.
  • Support enquiries: Messages and replies are kept for one year from the date received, then deleted. If you delete your account, the link between the enquiry and your account (user identifier) is severed, but the message itself remains for that period in case of a billing or refund dispute.
  • Reports: If you delete your account, the link to your account is removed and the report content is kept to improve quality.
  • Feature usage counts: Deleted when a signed-in user deletes their account.
  • Guest mode: Information linked to a guest user ID is deleted 13 months after last use. To delete it sooner, send a request via Settings → Message the developer. Messages and reports are kept with the ID link removed. If you sign in later, guest information is not merged and is deleted under the same rule.

5. App Permissions

  • Microphone / Speech recognition: Used only for voice word entry. Speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition. The App does not store speech or send it to our servers.
  • Camera: Used only when scanning photos to add words. Captured images are discarded immediately after text recognition and are not stored.
  • Photo library (optional): Used only when you import a photo for word extraction. Only the photo you select is processed.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used only to show ads to users without Pro. Pro users see no ads.
  • iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA): The App asks for tracking permission.

5-1. Pro Subscription Billing and Refunds

  • Payment processing: Pro subscriptions are billed exclusively through the store you installed from (Google Play on Android, the App Store on iOS). Payment instruments (cards, etc.) are handled by that store.
  • What we store: Only the purchase token (Android) or transaction identifier (iOS), the product ID, and the expiry date needed to activate your subscription.
  • Cancellation: Android — Google Play app → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. iOS — Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. You can cancel at any time.
  • Refunds: Refunds follow the policy of the store you purchased from (Google Play or the App Store) and are requested through that store.
  • After cancellation: Your vocabulary lists and study data remain intact (automatically reverting to Free tier limits).

6. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Restrict processing or withdraw your consent
  • Delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete account; guests: request via Settings → Message the developer)
  • If you are in the EEA/UK: rights under GDPR, including data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority
  • If you are in California: rights under CCPA, including the right to know, delete, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not "sell" personal information as defined by CCPA.

7. Age of Users and Children's Privacy

This service is intended for users aged 14 and older. The App does not knowingly collect personal information (including date of birth or age) from children under 14.

If we learn that a child under 14 has created an account, we will delete that account and related information promptly. You can report suspected cases via Settings → Message the developer in the App.
